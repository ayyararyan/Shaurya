retained as a replication/stress diagnostic rather than substituted for the authoritative
clean run; its identical 4,980-row / 2,491-burst count is reported without hiding the
incident.

**Cadence classification:** depth200 is a **quantized, skip-prone metronome, not dispersed
like Quote/Full**. It has a strong approximately 200 ms base/modal interval, but the p95 is
approximately 401 ms and the maximum approximately 603 ms, consistent with skipped base
ticks. It is clocked like depth20, but unlike depth20's single fixed 500 ms interval it is
not a guaranteed 200 ms publication.

**Mechanism:** every row lacks an exchange timestamp and source sequence. The tape identifies
the receive-side delivery cadence only; it cannot distinguish a Dhan publication interval,
broker-side aggregation, or an NSE-side snapshot. Mechanism category: **UNIDENTIFIED**.

dispersed, no single interval is guaranteed; both the measured median-gap lower bound and a
