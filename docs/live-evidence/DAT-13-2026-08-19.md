# DAT-13 live evidence — 2026-08-19

**Object measured:** packet delivery by security on one Dhan 200-level socket when the same four
front-month index futures are subscribed in different orders. Read-only market data only.

**Windows:** two 40-second observations completed at 10:20:41 and 10:21:43 IST, 2026-08-19.
The instruments were NIFTY (`58072`), BANKNIFTY (`58067`), FINNIFTY (`58070`), and MIDCPNIFTY
(`58071`) August 2026 futures, all expiring 2026-08-25.

| Subscription order | NIFTY packets | BANKNIFTY packets | FINNIFTY packets | MIDCPNIFTY packets | All received | Max/min skew |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---:|---:|
| NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY | 328 | 2 | 2 | 2 | yes | 164× |
| BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY, NIFTY | 2 | 328 | 2 | 2 | yes | 164× |

**Conclusion:** a genuine **first-subscribed-instrument throttle/bias** is present under this
four-instrument 200-level setup. The high-rate stream followed subscription position when the
order was rotated; it did not remain with NIFTY. Ordinary activity differences therefore cannot
explain the 328-versus-2 skew. “All received” is technically true but operationally misleading:
later instruments received only two packets each in 40 seconds.

**Unmeasured:** the exact multi-instrument cap, whether later instruments receive periodic
snapshots or a different packet subtype, how the throttle changes with socket/account count, and
whether one socket per 200-level instrument restores full event-rate delivery. Until measured,
multi-instrument 200-level sockets must not be treated as equivalent full-rate feeds.
