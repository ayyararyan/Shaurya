# DAT-15 live evidence — 2026-08-19

## Measurement object and identification boundary

The observed object is the age of the older BBO leg in DAT-14's latest complete causal depth
state when a Standard Quote/Full print arrives. The flip statistic compares the recorded causal
side with the side under the **first complete same-tier depth BBO received after the print within
1 second**.

That comparison is a diagnostic **proxy**, not identified truth. Dhan deep packets carry neither
exchange timestamps nor source sequence, and the post-print BBO may already reflect the trade.
The true exchange-time prevailing quote and true signing error are unidentified from this feed.

## Tape coverage

All five retained tapes plus all three captures made today were consumed: **8 tapes, 38,572 rows,
482 positive-volume prints**. The retained set includes one preserved zero-row invalid depth200
attempt, one 366-row depth200-only tape, two depth20-only probe tapes totalling 20,494 rows, the
419-row mixed tape with 12 prints, today's two healthy 10-minute DAT-14 tapes (11,691 rows, 313
prints), and a new 5,602-row/157-print simultaneous Standard+depth20+depth200 tape.

The last tape completed with every channel present but was operationally **degraded**: 39
reconnect boundaries and 36 heartbeat timeouts. DAT-14 correctly cleared quote state at every gap.
Results are therefore shown both for the seven-tape healthy core and for all eight tapes.

## Variation by quote age, activity, tier, and time

Across all tapes, directional proxy flips by causal quote-age band were:

| Quote-age band | Directional comparisons | Proxy flips | Rate |

| ≤100 ms | 137 | 1 | 0.73% |

| >500 ms | 9 | 0 | 0% |

The relationship is not monotone. The >500 ms denominator collapses because 40 stale prints are
explicitly unclassified, so zero flips there is not evidence that very stale quotes are safe.

**Activity.** The moderate BANKNIFTY tape (9.31 prints/min, n=93) had age median 209.7 ms/p95
436.4 ms, 0/93 flips, and 29.0% coalescing. Healthy active NIFTY tapes (n=232) had median 248.3
ms/p95 464.1 ms, 5/227 flips (2.20%), and 39.2% coalescing. The stressed active NIFTY tape adds
all 40 stale classifications, so activity and operational health are confounded in the all-tape
active band.

**Depth tier.** In the stressed cross-tier tape, depth20 was selected for 57 prints and depth200
for 100. Depth20 age median/p95 was 158.8 ms/8.81 s with 17 degraded; depth200 was 143.9 ms/9.97 s
with 23 degraded. Both had 0 observed proxy flips among 35 and 74 directional comparisons. This
does **not** establish a general tier ranking: both samples come from one reconnect-heavy run, and
no healthy Standard+depth200 tape exists.

**Time of day.** The morning sample has 470 prints; its all-tape age median/p95 is 211.8 ms/4.63 s
and flips are 3/420 (0.71%). Excluding the stressed run, the morning reference is n=313, median
238.7 ms/p95 462.6 ms, 3/311 flips (0.96%). The afternoon sample is only n=12: median 272.3
ms/p95 426.9 ms and 2/9 flips (22.2%). There is no midday sample. The afternoon rate is too small
to interpret as a stable time-of-day effect.
